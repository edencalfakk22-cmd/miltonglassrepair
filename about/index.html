About Sixteen Mile Glass Co.

Half of Milton has been standing since the 1800s and the other half went up in the last twenty years, and Sixteen Mile Glass Co. spends most weeks going back and forth between the two. A morning on Main Street easing a display lite out of a wood-stopped frame that has not been square since the war; an afternoon in Hawthorne Village or Coates where the sealed units that arrived with the subdivision are all reaching the end of their seals within a few years of each other. Neither job is harder than the other. They just want different hands and different stock on the truck.

The one habit we do not break is measuring. Every opening in this town has moved — the old frames from a century of settling, the new ones from a house that is still drying out — so we take the dimension off the opening as it sits today, order to that, and glaze it once. Insurance certificates and WSIB clearance go to a plaza landlord or property manager before we attend, not after they chase us for them. And if the house is still inside its Tarion new-home warranty we will say so and send you to your builder first, because that conversation is worth having before you pay anybody.

Why Milton property owners trust us

  • Boarded and secured the same evening, so Main Street opens on time the next morning
  • Heritage frames re-puttied and repaired rather than ripped out for an aluminum lookalike
  • Every dimension taken off the opening as it stands today, never off an original drawing
  • Still inside your Tarion warranty? We say so and point you back to the builder

Insured & WSIB-covered

Liability insurance and WSIB coverage are in place before a pane leaves the truck, and the certificates go to your property manager or landlord on request. Glass in a door, beside a door, or low enough for someone to walk into leaves here as tempered or laminated — that is not an upsell, it is what the position is required to have, in Milton and everywhere else in Halton.
