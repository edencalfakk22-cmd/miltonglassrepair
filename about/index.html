About Sixteen Mile Glass Co.

Sixteen Mile Glass Co. is a Milton glass and glazing outfit built around the two calls that actually matter: the one at two in the morning when a storefront is wide open on Main Street, and the one on a Tuesday about a fogged bedroom window in Hawthorne Village. Both get answered by the same crew, and both get the same treatment — we come and look at it, measure it ourselves, and put back the glass the opening is supposed to have.

We work right across Milton and Halton Region: downtown heritage frontages, the plazas along Louis St. Laurent and Bronte Street South, the subdivisions still being built out in Ford, Walker and Cobban, the warehouse and office units through Derry Green, and the older streets in Bronte Meadows, Dorset Park and Timberlea. We are fully insured and WSIB-covered, we carry the certificates property managers and landlords ask for, and we guarantee our workmanship in writing. What we will not do is invent credentials: Ontario issues no provincial trade licence for glass and glazing, so we tell you what we actually carry instead of quoting a number that does not exist.

Why Milton property owners trust us

  • 24/7 board-up, so a smashed opening is closed before morning trade
  • Safety glazing to code — never plain glass in a door or sidelite, never ordinary glass in a fire door
  • One crew from measure to install, with the board and the broken unit taken away
  • Insured and WSIB-covered, with the paperwork property managers and landlords ask for

Insured & WSIB-covered

We carry full liability insurance and WSIB coverage, and we replace door, sidelite and low-level glazing with the tempered or laminated safety glass the opening is required to have — serving property owners across Milton and the Greater Toronto Area.
