FAQs

Broken Window Glass & Emergency Board-Up — your questions

Our house is new — should we be calling the builder instead of you?

Quite possibly, and we would rather say so than take the work. New homes in Ontario carry Tarion warranty coverage, and damage or defects inside that period are usually the builder's to sort out. Call them first. If it turns out not to be covered, or you simply want it done now and settled later, we will do the work and give you documentation you can use either way.

The glass was damaged by trades during construction — what do we do?

Photograph it, tell the site supervisor before anything is replaced, and get the damage recorded while it is still attributable. We can attend, document what we find and quote the replacement, which is generally what a builder or a contractor wants in front of them. Replacing it quietly first makes the conversation about who pays much harder.

It is only a small chip at the edge — can it be left?

We would not leave it on an exposed pane. An edge chip concentrates stress at exactly the point where thermal cracks begin, so on a south or west elevation it tends to become a full crack in the first hard winter. A chip in the middle of the field of glass is far less urgent. Show us where it is and we will tell you honestly which of those you have.

Do you take the broken glass away?

Always. It leaves with us rather than going into your bin or your builder's skip, and we go over the ground before we pack up — fragments travel a long way, and on a new build there is usually landscaping fabric and mulch for them to hide in.
