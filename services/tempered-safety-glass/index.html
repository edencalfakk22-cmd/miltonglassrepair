FAQs

Tempered & Safety Glass Cut to Size — your questions

What is the difference between tempered and laminated glass?

Tempered glass is heat-treated so it breaks into small blunt pellets instead of shards. Laminated glass is two panes bonded to a plastic interlayer, so when it cracks the pieces stay stuck to the interlayer and the opening stays covered. Guards and overhead glazing usually call for laminated glass for exactly that reason.

Can tempered glass be cut down on site?

No. Once glass is tempered it cannot be cut, drilled or edge-worked — any attempt shatters the whole pane. Every dimension, hole and notch has to be right before fabrication, which is why we come out and measure the opening ourselves rather than taking sizes over the phone.

Do glass balcony and deck guards need special glass?

Yes. Ontario's supplementary standard SB-13 has required heat-soaked tempered or laminated glass in guards since 2012, after a run of guard-glass breakages on Ontario buildings was traced largely to nickel sulfide inclusions in ordinary tempered glass. We specify guard replacements to that standard, not to whatever the original panel happened to be.
