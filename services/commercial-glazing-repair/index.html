Commercial Glazing & Aluminum Frame Repair — your questions

Can you work outside our business hours?

Yes — before opening, after close, or overnight. Most Milton retail and warehouse glazing we do is scheduled around the operation rather than through it, and emergency board-up runs 24/7 regardless. If we need an escort, a lockup contact or a loading dock booked, we sort that out when we quote, not on the morning of.

Do you carry insurance and WSIB paperwork for our property manager?

Yes. Certificate of insurance naming the property owner or corporation, plus a WSIB clearance certificate, sent ahead of the visit so nothing stalls at the door. Ontario has no provincial trade licence for glazing, so this documentation — not a made-up licence number — is what you should be asking any glazier for.

Can a damaged aluminum frame be repaired instead of replaced?

Usually. Most of what fails on a Milton storefront is the consumables — gaskets, setting blocks, weatherstrip, the threshold and the pivots — not the aluminum itself, and those are all replaceable in place. If a member is bent, corroded through or has pulled apart at the corner, we tell you plainly and quote the section that genuinely needs replacing.
